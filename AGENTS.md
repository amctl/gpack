## Repository map

- Start at `README.md` for project status and document links.
- The accepted v1 product specification is in `docs/product/spec.md`.
- The format contract is in `docs/format/v1.md`; architecture and risks are
  in `docs/product/architecture.md` and `docs/product/risks.md`.
- Research evidence, current findings, and experiment status are indexed in `docs/research/README.md`.
- `.scratch/` contains planning issues; `docs/research/experiments/` contains research protocols and results.
- `goose-bin/` is an ignored local Goose toolchain used as research input.
